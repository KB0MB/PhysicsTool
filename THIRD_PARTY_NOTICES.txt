MeshCodec helper
---------------
The standalone PhysicsTool.MeshCodec executable calls dt-12345/MeshCodec's
DecompressMC API directly, using a PhysicsTool GPL-2.0-only stdin/stdout adapter.
The former Toolbox native wrapper is not included. Upstream is GPL-2.0;
the bundled source preserves the Zstandard, meshoptimizer-derived and float16
notices. Source revision and modifications are recorded in LOCAL_CHANGES.md.
https://github.com/dt-12345/MeshCodec
PhysicsTool communicates with this separate executable through standard I/O.
Complete corresponding helper source and licenses are included in
Licenses/MeshCodec-source.zip. Build instructions are in that archive's README.
