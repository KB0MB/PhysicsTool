BotW uking_mat shaders
Author: Killz

The original 20 shader files supplied to the project in BOTW Shader.zip are
preserved verbatim in src/PhysicsTool.Desktop/Rendering/Shaders/BotwNative.
PhysicsTool provides the shader adapter, material bindings and preview lighting.

On 2026-11-21 the project maintainer confirmed Killz approved distribution
of these sources in the public PhysicsTool repository and release ZIPs.
No separate license text was supplied. This attribution records that permission;
it does not assign an additional license to Killz's shader files.

Thank you to Killz for reverse-engineering and sharing the BotW shaders.
