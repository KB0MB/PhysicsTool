TotK material shaders
Author: Killz

The GLSL supplied by Killz in TOTK.zip is preserved in
src/PhysicsTool.Desktop/Rendering/Shaders/TotkNative.
PhysicsTool provides the desktop shader adapter, material bindings and preview
lighting. Killz's original shader sources are not authored by PhysicsTool.

These sources were supplied for integration into PhysicsTool. No separate
license text accompanied the archive; this notice does not assign an additional
license to Killz's files.
